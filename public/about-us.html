🌟 About Us

The Chosen Generation is a Children's Bible School dedicated to nurturing the spiritual growth of children through engaging, age-appropriate Bible education. We believe every child is uniquely created by God with a special purpose, and our mission is to help them discover their identity in Christ.

Our program is built on three foundational pillars: Family - creating a supportive community where children feel loved and valued; Identity - helping children understand who they are in Christ; and Purpose - equipping them to fulfill God's unique calling for their lives.

"Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it." - Proverbs 22:6

👁️ Our Vision

To raise a generation of children who are deeply rooted in God's Word, confident in their identity as children of God, and equipped to impact their families, communities, and the world for Christ.

We envision children who not only know the Bible but live it out daily - young disciples who understand their worth, walk in purpose, and become leaders in their generation.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide a fun, safe, and spiritually enriching environment where children can:

  • Learn and memorize God's Word through interactive lessons and activities
  • Develop a personal relationship with Jesus Christ

  • Form meaningful friendships with other believers
  • Grow in prayer and worship

📋 Our Approach

We follow a gradual, intentional approach to ensure children and families are properly introduced to our ministry and prepared for deeper spiritual growth.

1 Monthly Meetings (Introduction Phase)

We start here! Our journey begins with monthly gatherings where we introduce ourselves to the children and their families. These meetings are designed to be fun, engaging, and welcoming, allowing children to get comfortable with our team and environment.

  • Fun introductory activities and games
  • Simple Bible stories and songs
  • Meet and greet with teachers and volunteers
  • Parent orientation and Q&A sessions
  • Registration for ongoing participation

2 Transition Period (2-3 Meetings)

After the initial meetings, we spend time getting to know each child personally. During this phase, we collect important information and build relationships.

  • Collect bio data and emergency contact information
  • Gather parent/guardian details
  • Assess each child's spiritual background
  • Introduce foundational Bible concepts
  • Prepare children for Foundation School enrollment

3 Foundation School (Ongoing Program)

After 2-3 successful monthly meetings and proper registration, children transition into our structured Foundation School program with regular weekly/bi-weekly sessions.

  • Structured curriculum with levels (FS1, FS2, FS3)
  • Weekly lessons with memory verses
  • Points and badge reward system
  • Progress tracking for parents
  • Special events and graduation ceremonies

📚 Foundation School Curriculum

Our structured curriculum is divided into progressive levels:

Foundation School 1 (FS1) Ages 4-6: Basic Bible stories, simple prayers, learning about Jesus' love, introductory worship
Foundation School 2 (FS2) Ages 7-9: Deeper Bible study, memory verses, understanding salvation, prayer development
Foundation School 3 (FS3) Ages 10-12: Character building, leadership skills, personal devotion, service projects

Progress Tracking: Children earn points for attendance, completing tasks, memorizing verses, and good behavior. Parents can track progress through the parent portal.

💻 Our Digital Platform

We've built a modern, child-friendly digital platform to enhance the learning experience:

  • Parent Portal: Track your children's progress, view their points, see upcoming sessions, and communicate with teachers
  • Child Dashboard: Fun, colorful interface where children can check in, view tasks, see their badges, and track their journey
  • Teacher Portal: Teachers can award points, track attendance, and manage their classes
  • Admin Portal: Full management of all children, classes, and curriculum
  • Secure PIN Login: Each child gets a unique 4-digit PIN for easy, secure access
  • Points & Badges: Gamified reward system to motivate and celebrate achievements

🛡️ Safety & Safeguarding

The safety of every child is our highest priority. We maintain strict safeguarding policies:

  • All volunteers and teachers undergo background checks
  • Secure check-in/check-out procedures using unique PINs
  • Adult-to-child ratios maintained at all times
  • Medical information and emergency contacts on file
  • Photo/video consent obtained from parents
  • Clear reporting procedures for any concerns
  • Regular safeguarding training for all team members
